Trainer's Boardroom · Guide

Play a full season, then decide

Every new career in Trainer's Boardroom starts with a free season. No subscription, no ads, no three-day trial that runs out before the first derby.

Until 31 October: free for good

Install Trainer's Boardroom by 31 October and the full game stays free for you, for good.

What the free season includes

The whole first season with everything the game has: league and cup, transfers and contracts, live matches you can change from the touchline, and a board that holds you to your target. None of it is cut down.

What comes after

If you want to carry on after that first season, one purchase unlocks the full game: €6.99, shown in your currency. No subscription and nothing that buys you an edge: paying decides whether you keep playing, never how strong your club is. Optional extras that arrive later are sold separately.

What if I start again?

Every new career starts with another free season, so you can try a small club and a big one before you make up your mind.

Offline, even while you try it

The free season runs entirely without internet and without an account. You only need Google Play once, for the purchase itself.

It's early access, so there are still rough edges, and it grows with every update. Moving to another club during a career arrives in 2027.

Get it on Google Play

In English, German and Croatian. Android now, iPhone in 2027.
