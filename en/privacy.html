Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The app „Trainer’s Boardroom“

Playing the game involves no personal data. Data only leaves your device for the services described one by one below: Google Play Games if you have a profile there and, from version 1.7, our own small service for membership and studio messages and, only if you say yes, for “Help improve the start”, comparing with other managers and bug reports. We have no user accounts and no login of our own. The app runs fully offline. You do not need an internet connection to play; only the update check, the look for studio messages at launch, keeping your membership in step if you are a member and, if you have said yes to them, sending start-of-game steps, season results and bug reports (see below) use the network, and without a connection the app starts as usual. Your entire save, meaning your career, squad, finances and results, lives on your device in a local database. A copy only leaves your device if you use the cloud save in Google Play Games (it then sits in your Google Account, not with us) or if you send us your save with a bug report (see “Bug reports with your save”). The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools from other companies. Only if you say yes does our own service count at which of the first steps new players drop out (see “Help improve the start”).

Updates: At launch the app asks the update service of Expo (Expo, Inc., USA; GDPR compliant, certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) whether a newer version of the game code is available, and downloads it in the background if so. What is transmitted is the platform, the version number, the release channel and a random installation number that is not tied to your device: no device identifier, no name, no save data. Your IP address is processed technically for the transfer and is not stored by us. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a working app). Without an internet connection the app starts and plays as usual.

Our own service (from version 1.7): From version 1.7 the app talks to a small service that we run ourselves on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). Its database is held in the European Union. You never need it to play: offline, or if it does not answer, the game carries on as normal and sends anything outstanding later. With each request the app sends its version number and the language you play in, but no device ID, no advertising ID and no location; a random installation ID or your save only goes where you allow it below. Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the request; our service uses it for no more than a minute to turn away floods of requests from one address, and does not store it. The service writes no personal data to its logs. Cloudflare acts as our processor under its data processing agreement, which includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, and is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in a secure, reliable service).

Studio messages (from version 1.6): If you are online when the game starts, the app fetches the studio’s messages, for example “Version 1.8 is out” or “We’ve found a bug and are working on a fix”, and shows them in the main menu. From version 1.7 it asks our own service first (see above) and only falls back to a small file on our website, trainersboardroom.com, which is served through GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA); version 1.6 uses the file only. Nothing is sent apart from the request itself: no device ID, no name, no save data. Your IP address is processed only to deliver the file, and we do not store it. Which messages you have dismissed is remembered on your device only. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in telling players about new versions and known bugs). Offline, the app starts and plays as usual.

Ratings: After special achievements in the game, the app may once per career ask Google Play to show its rating dialog. The dialog comes from Google Play and is subject to Google’s privacy policy; the app itself receives no data from it.

Purchases: You can unlock the full game with a one-off purchase. Google Play handles the whole purchase, and the app never receives your name, your address or your payment details. It asks Google Play whether the Google account on this device owns the purchase, checks Google’s signature on the answer, acknowledges the purchase to Google Play as Google requires, and keeps the result on your device. Whether you installed the app before 1 November 2026, and so keep playing without buying, is also worked out on your device: from the first-install date that Android provides, from any existing saves and from a small marker the app keeps on your device for this purpose. If you have backup switched on, Android includes that marker, and nothing else from the app, in its backup to your Google account. All it says is that the app was installed in time, so the game stays unlocked if you reinstall it or set up a new device from your backup. Google runs the backup under its own privacy policy, and we have no access to it. Your saves are not included. In the European Union the seller is Google Commerce Limited (Ireland); the purchase is subject to Google’s terms and privacy policy. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (unlocking the game you bought). From version 1.7 our service also checks this purchase with Google Play (see “Membership”).

Google Play Games (from version 1.2.1): From version 1.2.1 the app uses Google Play Games services (Google Ireland Limited) for three things: the founder note, achievements and the cloud save for your career. If you have a Play Games profile and let Play Games sign you in to games automatically, the app signs in quietly when it starts. If you don’t have a profile, nothing happens; the app only offers a button in its settings so you can sign in if you choose to.

Founder note: If you installed the app before 1 November 2026, the game should stay free for you even after a reinstall without a backup, or on a new phone. For this the app keeps one saved game called “Trainer’s Boardroom” in your Play Games account, holding nothing but that fact.

Achievements: When you reach certain goals in the game (your first win or a league title, for example), the app reports the achievement to Play Games, where it shows in your profile.

Cloud save: While it is switched on (the default; you can turn it off under Settings → Cloud save), the app backs up your career to your Play Games account after you play, at most every 15 minutes and only over Wi-Fi unless you allow mobile data. What is backed up is the complete save of that career (club, squad, finances, results), described by the club name, season and in-game date. After a reinstall or on a new device, the app asks whether you want to restore it.

The app does not read your Play Games name, your profile picture or your email address. We receive none of this data; it stays in your Google Account. According to Google, Play Games itself processes your gamer identity (your profile name and picture), saved games, achievements, and analytics and diagnostics data used to keep its services stable; Google’s privacy policy applies to that. You can view and delete saved games, achievements and all your other Play Games data in your Play Games profile or your Google Account, and a backed-up career also in the game when you delete a save (“Cloud copy too”). Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (keeping the unlock we promised you and providing the features you use).

Membership (from version 1.7): You buy a membership (Bronze, Silver or Gold) through Google Play, just like the game itself, and we never receive your name, address or payment details. So that we can give you a real membership number, the app sends our service the purchase token that Google Play issues for the purchase, along with the order number where there is one. Our service uses the token to ask Google Play whether the purchase is valid, and Google tells it the product, the time and status of the purchase and the country it was made in. We keep the token only as a one-way hash (SHA-256), together with the order number, product, purchase time and country, and give you a running membership number. It is shown on your member card in the app and is never given to anyone else. For every new purchase and every refund we get an email with the tier, the number and the country, but no name; it is sent through Brevo (see section 4). Once a day our service asks Google which purchases have been refunded. Unlocking still happens on your device through Google Play: our service never takes away anything Google Play has given you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the membership you bought). Reading the purchase token on your device is strictly necessary for this service, which you asked for (Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive; in Germany Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). We keep the data for as long as you are a member; after a refund we delete it after 30 days. From version 1.7 our service checks a purchase of the full game in the same way, just without a membership number; we keep those details for as long as the purchase stands, and for 30 days after a refund.

Roll of honour (from version 1.7): As a Silver or Gold member you can choose to put a display name of your own on the roll of honour in the app. The app then sends that name and your consent to our service. We look at every name before it goes up and turn down offensive names and other people’s real names; it appears 48 hours after the purchase at the earliest. From then on it can be seen by every player worldwide, with your tier, your membership number and the date you joined. Pick a made-up name if you would rather not appear under your real one. If you withdraw the name in the app, our service deletes it straight away, and it disappears for other players the next time they open the roll of honour while online. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time, with effect for the future, by withdrawing the name. After a refund the name comes off the roll of honour as soon as our service picks up the refund (by the next day at the latest) and is deleted after 30 days.

Gold wish (from version 1.7): Gold members can ask, from within the app, to appear in the game as a youth player or manager at a club. The app sends our service what you enter in the form (youth player or manager, the club, the name you want in the game and, if you like, an email address for our reply) together with your consent. We receive the wish by email through Brevo and on our admin page. If we grant it, the name joins the game world, including careers already under way. If you withdraw your consent (an email to trainersboardroom@outlook.com is enough) or the purchase is refunded, the name disappears from the game everywhere again, careers under way included, as soon as each copy of the game hears about it (the next time it syncs while online). Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). We keep a granted wish for as long as the name is in the game and a declined one for 90 days; if you withdraw it, we delete it straight away. We keep the email about it for no more than a year.

Members’ vote (from version 1.7): Silver and Gold members can vote on one question about the game with each version. The app sends your choice together with the purchase token; we store the vote against the hash described under “Membership”, so that every member has exactly one vote and can change it until the vote closes. We never show anyone an individual vote; everyone sees only the overall result. 30 days after a vote closes we keep only the totals. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (part of your membership).

Help improve the start (from version 1.7): If you say yes in the app, it tells our service when you first reach each of ten fixed steps: saying yes itself, starting a new game, picking a club, looking at the squad, your first moment in the office, your first day played, your first match, your first month in the game, and coming back on the second and on the seventh day. Each step comes with the time since the app was opened, which session it is and whether this is your first career, plus a random installation ID that the app creates the first time it sends something. It is not a device ID or an advertising ID and is not linked to any name. What you do in the game (line-ups, transfers, money) is not sent. This shows us where new players drop out, so we can improve the start at exactly that point; every Monday we get an email with nothing but numbers. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for storing and reading the ID on your device, Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive, in Germany Section 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time under Settings → Data & privacy; the app then sends nothing more and drops anything not yet sent, and our service straight away deletes what it already holds under your ID. We keep the individual steps for 90 days and after that only weekly totals with no ID.

Compare with other managers (from version 1.7): Everyone starts in the same world. That lets the app show, when it looks back on each of your first three seasons, how other managers got on with the same club: how many won the league and where they finished. Everyone can see this. You only count towards it if you say yes: after each of your first three seasons the app then sends your club, the season, your league position, the trophies you won (league, cup, Europe), the version of the starting world and the installation ID from “Help improve the start”. A club only shows up once ten careers have been counted, and only as percentages, so nobody can be picked out. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive, in Germany Section 25(1) TDDDG), which you can withdraw at any time under Settings → Data & privacy; the app then sends nothing more, and our service straight away deletes your individual results. We keep the individual results for a year and after that only the totals per club, season and position, with no ID.

Bug reports with your save (from version 1.7): With the bug report in Settings you can send us a bug together with your save, so we can play through exactly your career. With your consent in the form, our service receives your description, an email address for our reply if you want one, the game version, your phone model, your Android version and your packed save. The save holds your whole career; the manager name you typed in yourself can turn up in it in news items and awards, as can the roll-of-honour names of other members that everyone sees in the game anyway. It is sent over Wi-Fi, and over mobile data only if you allow it. The save is kept in Cloudflare’s file storage in the European Union; we get an email through Brevo with the report number, your description, the reply address and the technical details, but not the save itself. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). We delete a report that was started but not fully sent after 24 hours and a complete one after 60 days; we keep the email about it for no more than a year. If you want a report deleted sooner, email trainersboardroom@outlook.com with the number the app shows after sending.

Sharing: Only as described above: with Expo (updates), with Google (purchases, refunds and Play Games) and with our processors Cloudflare and Brevo (our service with its database and file storage, and its emails). Once approved, display names on the roll of honour can be seen by all players; the comparison with other managers shows everyone percentages only, from ten careers per club. We do not sell data and pass nothing on for advertising.

Deletion: Because your data lives only on your device, you can remove it yourself at any time: through your device’s app settings, through the reset button in the game (Settings → Reset game), or by uninstalling the app. A career backed up to the cloud can be deleted in the game when you delete a save (“Cloud copy too”), or in your Play Games profile or Google Account. What our service stores about a membership (the paragraphs from “Membership” to “Members’ vote”) is not on your device, so uninstalling the app does not delete it. We delete all of it on request: email trainersboardroom@outlook.com with the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with “GPA.”). As there are no user accounts, that number is the only way we can find your data. So that the deletion sticks, we then keep only the one-way hash of the purchase, with no number, country or purchase time; if the app reports the same purchase again, after a reinstall for example, we no longer set up a membership for it. Whatever Google Play unlocks on your device stays unlocked. Your other rights (access, rectification, restriction, objection and complaining to a supervisory authority) are set out in section 6 and apply to the app as well. Start-of-game steps and season results are tied only to the random installation ID, which the app never shows, so we cannot match them to a person. If you switch off “Help improve the start” or “Compare with other managers” under Settings → Data & privacy, or reset the ID, our service straight away deletes everything it holds under that ID; the comparison keeps only totals with no ID. We delete a bug report straight away on request: email trainersboardroom@outlook.com with the number the app showed after sending.

Permissions: The app requests only the permissions needed to run and to store its local save. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files outside its own storage.

Children: Playing the game involves no data from anyone, children included. Anyone under 16 needs a parent’s consent to put a name on the roll of honour, to send a Gold wish or to send a bug report, and also to say yes to “Help improve the start” and to comparing with other managers. Purchases go through Google Play and its family rules, and Google’s own age rules apply to Google Play Games.

On the Lozanite Studio website you can put yourself on a waiting list or subscribe to the newsletter. Which personal data are transmitted follows from the input form, as a rule only the e-mail address. The newsletter can only be received if the data subject has a valid e-mail address and registers for the mailing. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent).

Suggestions and messages. Brevo delivers both to our Outlook.com (Microsoft) inbox as an e-mail. How the forms reach Brevo. Neither form sends your details to Brevo directly. Cloudflare acts as our processor under its data processing addendum, which includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, and is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in accepting the forms securely without exposing access credentials on the website).
