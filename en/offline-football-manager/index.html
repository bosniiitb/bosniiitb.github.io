An offline football manager for Android

No signal needed, no subscription, no adverts before kick-off. Here's what to look for in a football manager you can play anywhere, and how Trainer's Boardroom handles it.

Does it really work offline?

Plenty of games say offline and still want a connection to show ads between matches. Airplane mode tells you in seconds. Trainer's Boardroom needs no internet and no account: the whole world runs on your phone.

Subscription or one-off?

There's no subscription. From November, every new career starts with a free season, and after that the full game is €6.99, paid once and shown in your currency. Install by 31 October and it's free for good.

Ads or pay-to-win?

No ads, and nothing that buys you an edge. Optional extras that arrive later are sold separately.

Enough depth for a long career?

100 leagues in 51 countries, 1,459 clubs, domestic cups and three European competitions. Transfers come in two steps, the fee with the club and then wages and length with the player, plus loans and instalments. Matches play out minute by minute and you can make changes from the touchline. Above you sits a board that holds you to your target.

Built for the commute

Play a matchday, make one transfer call, put the phone away. Your save stays on the device and picks up where you left off.

It's early access, so there are still rough edges, and it grows with every update. Moving to another club during a career arrives in 2027.

Get it on Google Play

In English, German and Croatian. Android now, iPhone in 2027.
