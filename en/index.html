Meet your new club

Hi, I'm Fabian, the developer behind Trainer's Boardroom. I'm building the football manager I always wanted to play: you take over a club and lead it season after season. You pick the lineup, negotiate transfers, keep the finances in check and fight for promotion or against relegation.

The difference: you don't just read a result. You follow your match live and step in mid-game. Your progress is saved, so you play whenever you have a moment.

The heart of it

You're on the touchline

No result to read off. You follow your match minute by minute: goals, cards, injuries. And when it turns, you step in. Change the formation, change the tactics, up to five substitutions, while the match is still running. No time? Hit Simulate and just take the result.

What makes it hard

Calibrated against real football

2.77 goals per match, measured, not claimed. Cards tuned to real league data, 54 researched league profiles, a loan system modelled on real football, market values calibrated to real fees.

The board can sack you

Let the debt sit too long and you're out, mid-season. “You should have sorted that out yourself.”

Scouting in ranges, not numbers

Your youth academy produces its own talents every year. Scouted players show only ranges at first. The better your club scouts, the sharper the picture.

Your players have character

Leave a player on the bench too long and he'll let you know. An established player won't go just anywhere, not even on loan to a small league. The dressing room is your job.

No small print

Where it stands

Trainer's Boardroom isn't finished, and I'd rather tell you before kick-off than in the small print: there are still bugs.

Most recently I worked through the club's finances from A to Z and calibrated them against real football: budgets, wages, fees, sponsors. Then the speed: a transfer day took up to twelve seconds on the phone, now one. There are still bugs. My list is long, and it gets shorter version by version.

If something feels off, do report it. I read every report and sort it in. Whatever makes the game better goes into one of the next updates. Beyond that I stick to my list, stubbornly: version by version.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can I play right now?

Yes. Trainer's Boardroom is on the Play Store as early access: download, pick your club, kick off. It isn't finished yet; it grows with every version.

Which version is live?

1.2, Europe. New in it: you play Europe yourself, from the qualifiers through group nights and knockout ties to a final at a neutral venue. Relegation play-offs follow each country's real format. The economy has been rebuilt: every club plans its season, crowds and revenue fit the league, and your ledger runs month by month. And you can sign prospects from other academies; when a bigger club comes for one of yours, you hear about it first. Plus everything from 1.1: the board with a voice, stadium, academy and scouting as rooms, training with a report, a market where the AI negotiates, the day computed in the background. Still missing: a manager career.

What does early access mean?

A game that runs but still has rough edges. Everything important is in: 100 leagues with promotion and relegation, domestic and European cups, live matches with tactical changes, negotiations with club and player, loans, instalments, a youth academy, scouting, training, players with character, injuries, suspensions, finances, sponsors and a budget from the board. Not in yet: a manager's career. And honestly: there are still bugs. If you meet one, write to me.

Does it cost anything?

Install by 31 October and you play for free, for good. From November, every new career starts with a free season, and after that the whole game costs 6.99 € once, shown in your own currency. No subscription, no pay-to-win, no ads, nothing else to buy. If you were in early and a reinstall suddenly asks you to pay, write to me and I will sort it out.

I have an iPhone.

Android is already out, on Google Play. iPhone comes after that, with no date yet. Sign up and I'll let you know.

Do I need internet to play?

Only to download it. After that everything runs on your phone, no account, no server. Play on the train, on a plane, wherever.

How do I report a bug?

Straight to trainersboardroom@outlook.com or on Reddit. What helps me: your device, what you were doing, and a screenshot if you can. I read every report myself.
